Download Android APK
Version 1.3.0 · Android 7.0 and newer · Signed release

Install in three steps

  1. Download the APK, then open it from your phone’s Downloads folder.
  2. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser or file manager. On Android 7, this setting is usually under Settings → Security → Unknown sources. Turn it off again after installation.
  3. Tap Install, open Cartlyft, and sign in with your existing account while connected to the internet.

Your business data uses the same account and database. You will sign in again in the app; browser sessions do not transfer automatically.

For order alerts, keep the app open. This release supports in-app alerts, but does not receive push notifications while closed. Opening inventory alarms can continue after logout when enabled on the phone. Changes saved from another phone stop the alarm only after this app refreshes online. Staff changes saved on the phone sync when internet returns. Customer orders need internet confirmation.

Recover a saved reconciliation

After updating on the same phone, sign in as the owner and open Reconcile → Recover saved reports. Review the date, cash and UPI figures, then choose Restore this report → Confirm restore. Recovery depends on a backup still existing on that phone. Do not uninstall or clear app storage.

Updating later

In version 1.2.0 and newer, open App settings → Check for updates → Download update. The APK downloads in the background. Return to Cartlyft and tap Install, then approve Android’s prompt. Older versions need this one-time download to get the new updater. Do not uninstall or clear app storage while changes are waiting to sync.

If the screen won’t load

Enable or update Android System WebView and Google Chrome in the Play Store, then reopen the app. Use the app’s ⋮ menu for Reload, printing, and updates.
